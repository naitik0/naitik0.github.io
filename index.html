A bit about me

I'm pursuing my MCA at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, after a BCA in Computer Science specializing in Cyber Security & Computer Networks from Galgotias University, which I completed with First Class Distinction.

Most of what I build sits where Python meets security. SecureSteg hides encrypted data inside images using AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2. As QA tester on the EduAdapt AI hackathon team, I found and patched a weak JWT secret that left authentication open to token forgery.

I've interned with CodSoft as a Python Programming intern, and completed AICTE virtual internships with AWS and Zscaler covering cloud architecture and Zero Trust security. I'm also an Oracle-certified Agentic AI Foundations Associate.
